I was a new car dealer, SCCA driver and restorer. After selling the last of my three collections (55 cars), I looked around to store my last remaining 8 cars and was disappointed with what I found. So if you’re looking for the best, centrally located, indoor auto storage, I encourage you to give me a call.

Restoration & maintenance

We never intended to be a restoration shop, but our customers insisted. David has restored cars for 40+ years and has won a number of national titles. We do not paint exteriors, but everything else is within our grasp. Painting, chroming and interiors go to skilled subcontractors he personally supervises. His experience centers on American cars from 1921 to 1976, and newer projects are welcome.

We finish what others started

Often a car is restored and the shop tires of it, or the owner tires of paying for it, and the details that make a car enjoyable daily are left undone. David’s goal is for you to drive your collector car, with a special expertise in making old cars usable in the modern world.

It is not unusual for us to take a finished, shiny car and remove transmissions, engines, window motors, dashes, rear ends and suspensions, make them new again and reinstall them without harming the original restoration.

A little history…

Photo: David Fitzgerald with a vintage collector car

I had my first collector car before I had a driver’s license. Since then, I’ve owned in excess of 150 cars, all drivable, showable vehicles. Out of that number, I’ve performed probably 30+ complete restorations with numerous other “paint and interior” restorations. All of the cars, with the exception of the exotics, I endeavored to maintain and improve myself.

My passion has always been for mechanical things. As a franchise new car dealer for 30+ years, I was able to indulge my passion. Business would demand that I would sell off my collections at times to raise capital for business ventures. My collections encompassed muscle, exotic, and classic.

My last collection encompassed 55 automobiles, and when I sold my dealership, it was time to sell my collection. With my remaining 8 cars, I started looking around for a storage facility and was not impressed. So, now in retirement, I decided to open a car hotel with me as the head butler. Every day I get to come to work at a car museum and people pay me for it.
